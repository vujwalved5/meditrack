08 GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers.
Clearer operations.

MediTrack brings equipment records, availability, department allocation, check-out activity, maintenance flags and audit history into one workspace.

A QR code identifies an equipment record, helping staff quickly review or update an item's status during check-out and return workflows.

The audit log lets managers search equipment or users, filter by department and action, and export activity history.
